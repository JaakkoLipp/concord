# M4.1 plan: UI alignment fixes

Status: signed off by Jaakko on 2026-10-15, built. Implements M4.1 in [milestones.md](milestones.md), from Jaakko's screenshots of 2026-10-15. Measured on the local stack at 1600 and 1100 px wide.

## What's wrong, measured

| Problem | Measured | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| The composer's placeholder isn't centred | Its text sits 5 px above the box's centre | `.cm-content` has a fixed 12 px top padding (`editor.ts`), but the box is 58 px tall (`--composer-h`), so the extra height goes below the text |
| The dock and the composer aren't level | The composer's bottom edge is 8 px above the dock's: 884 against 892 | The composer leaves a 16 px strip below it for the typing indicator. The dock's margin is 8 px. |
| "… is typing" is cramped | That strip is 16 px and touches the window's bottom edge | `TypingIndicator.svelte`'s fixed height |

The server menu rendered with aligned text, so Jaakko's "text is not aligned" is read as the placeholder. Jaakko to say if something else was meant.

## Tasks

1. **Centre the composer's text**: one line of text sits in the middle of the box, and a growing message still grows downward from there. This also applies to the edit box.
2. **One bottom line**:
   - the typing strip gets its own row of a token height, `--space-7`,
   - the dock's bottom margin matches it, so the dock and the composer end on the same line,
   - the voice panel above the dock keeps its spacing.
3. **The typing indicator**: text vertically centred in its row, its left edge in line with the composer's text, and the dots' animation not touching the edge.
4. **A short review pass** at 1600 and 1100 px, and on the phone layout, of:
   - the composer with a reply, an attachment and the emoji picker open,
   - the dock with and without the voice panel,
   - DMs, a server channel and a voice channel's chat.

   Any alignment slip found is fixed in the same way, and a larger one is noted for M7.

Tokens only, no raw sizes.

**Done when**:
- screenshots before and after, at both widths, show the placeholder centred within 1 px, the dock and composer bottoms equal, and the typing row clear of the window's edge,
- `pnpm verify` passes,
- it is deployed to production for Jaakko's review.
