# M4.6 plan: moderation, voice and text

Status: draft for Jaakko's sign-off. Revised after a Sonnet challenger review and a Sonnet audit of every role permission in the client. Results go in `docs/m4.6-results.md`.

Asked for on 2026-10-15:
- dragging people between voice channels, as on Discord,
- server mute and deafen in a voice participant's right-click menu, for those whose role allows it,
- every permission in the role editor actually working,
- whatever else moderating servers and the app needs.

Working agreement, as before: each finished task is committed and pushed after `pnpm verify`, deployed to `/app`, and checked with `routes.ts`. Agents work on the local stack.

## What Stoat 0.15.5 gives us

A Sonnet agent read Stoat's source at the v0.15.5 tag without running anything. The key lines are in the appendix.

| Action | How | State in 0.15.5 |
|---|---|---|
| **Move** someone to another voice channel | Member edit with `voice_channel`, needing Move members, and Connect on the destination | **Works.** The target gets a new token in a private `UserMoveVoiceChannel` event, and everyone sees `VoiceChannelMove`. Our client doesn't handle the private event yet. |
| **Server mute, server deafen** | Member edit with `can_publish` or `can_receive`, needing Mute members or Deafen members | **Half works.** The member record changes and everyone is told, so the mark shows. The live call is updated for the moderator instead of the target. Server-side enforcement can't be had at all, even on rejoin: a token without Speak and Video carries no restriction on what may be published (architecture 5.4). |
| **Disconnect** from voice | Member edit with `remove: ["VoiceChannel"]` | **Broken.** It removes the moderator, or answers 500, and the target stays. |
| Kick, ban | Existing routes | Work, and drop the target from voice. A ban can also delete the user's messages from up to the last 7 days. |
| **Timeout** | Member edit with `timeout` | Works, with no maximum. A timed-out member can only read. |
| **Slow mode** | `slowmode`, 0 to 21,600 s, on a text channel | Works. Bypass slow mode is the exception. |
| **Deleting many messages** | `DELETE /channels/{c}/messages/bulk` | Works: up to 100, each under 7 days old, needing Manage messages. |
| **Audit log** | `GET /servers/{s}/audit_logs` | Works: 30 days kept, needing View audit logs. Each entry records a moderation action and, for edits, what changed. An `X-Audit-Log-Reason` header adds a reason. |
| Nicknames, server avatars | Member edit | Work: your own needs Change nickname or Change avatar, other people's needs Manage nicknames or Remove avatars. |
| Reports | `POST /safety/report` | **No use here.** Stored where no route reads them, so no moderator ever sees one. |

**Limits**: every `/servers/…` request, member edits and the audit log included, shares one bucket of 5 per 10 s per user and server. The client's queue waits for room, so several moves in a row take seconds.

## How voice moderation works without a Stoat fix

- **Move** uses Stoat's route as it is, and Concord clients follow the move.
- **Server mute and deafen** set the member's flags through Stoat. The target's Concord client acts on the change at once, as it already does for a mute set elsewhere:
  - a mute takes the microphone off the call and locks the button,
  - a deafen stops receiving audio and locks the button.

  Others see the mark from the same update. Stoat's own client and modified clients aren't bound by it. That's acceptable in this community, and the upstream fix closes it.
- **Disconnect** waits for the upstream fix. Doing it through LiveKit directly would mean giving `concord-web` the LiveKit secret, which can also sign tokens for any call, so anyone who took over the web server could listen in.

## Tasks

### 1. Probes

On the local stack, with `dev-admin` as the moderator:
- **Move**: what the moved client receives and when; whether a stale `VoiceChannelLeave` arrives; how LiveKit's removal from the old room looks to the client, so it isn't taken for a kick; what happens if the target is reconnecting.
- **Mute and deafen**, with the moderator in the same room, in another room and in no call: what the moderator's own call goes through because of Stoat's bug, and whether it needs repairing.
- **Disconnect**: confirm it's broken.
- **Timeout in a call**: does the member keep talking?
- **Ban with deletion**: which messages go, and how fast.
- **Without permissions**: what Stoat answers for history without Read message history, joining without Connect, publishing a camera without Video, and joining without Listen.

**Done when** each has an answer in m4.6-results.md.

### 2. Following a move

**Built**:
- **The controller handles `UserMoveVoiceChannel`.** It reconnects with the token Stoat sent, with no `join_call` of its own, keeping mute, deafen and volumes.
- **An expected move isn't a kick.** While a move is under way, the old room's removal doesn't read as "Disconnected". If no token comes, or it arrives after its 10 s life, the client falls back to an ordinary join.
- **Edge cases**: two moves in a row, and a move during a reconnect.
- **A toast** says who moved you and where.

**Done when** a live check moves `aino` back and forth between Olohuone and Pelaamo over the API, twice in quick succession, then once while her connection is reconnecting. Her call must follow each time without a click, send audio within 3 s, and send no extra `join_call`.

### 3. Voice moderation

**Built**:
- **Dragging** a person's row in the sidebar onto another voice channel, for someone with Move members.
  - Allowed destinations highlight, which needs Connect there for both of you.
  - A move still waiting for the rate limit shows as pending on the row, and is dropped if the person has left by then.
  - Screen readers hear the result.
  - On touch, a long press keeps opening the menu.
- **"Move to"** in the participant menu, listing the voice channels allowed, for keyboard and touch.
- **"Server mute" and "Server deafen"** as checkboxes in the participant menu, for those with Mute members or Deafen members.
- **Who the items show for**: only someone below you in rank, using the same check as kick and ban, and only while their call is in this server, never a DM call. Not on your own row.
- **On the target's side**: their mic or headphones turn red and locked, with "Server muted" or "Server deafened" as the tooltip. A server mute takes their microphone off the call, and gaining it back puts it on again, as Speak does.

**Done when** a live check has `dev-admin` do each of these to `aino`:
- drag her into another channel, and move her with "Move to" by keyboard,
- server-mute her: her audio falls to near zero within 2 s, `dev` sees the mark, and her mute button is locked,
- unmute her: her audio returns,
- deafen and undeafen her: what she receives stops and returns.

`dev`, without the permissions, sees none of the items.

### 4. Every permission does what it says

From the audit.

**Members see why they can't do something, rather than a blank screen or a misleading error:**
- **Read message history**: the channel shows "You can't read this channel's history" instead of opening blank.
- **Connect**: the voice channel and the call buttons say you can't join, rather than trying and failing.
- **Video**: the camera and share buttons are disabled with a reason, rather than "check that it's plugged in".
- **Listen**: the call says you can't hear this channel.
- **Speak**: the mute button is disabled with "You can't speak here".
- **Upload files**: the disabled "+" button gets its reason.

**Mentions**: `@everyone` and `@online` are suggested to those with Mention everyone, and a refused mention says why.

**A timeout running out** brings back reactions and uploads at once, not only the composer.

**Your own server profile**: "Edit server profile" in the server menu sets your nickname and server avatar, with Change nickname and Change avatar.

**The role editor**:
- View audit log is added.
- A channel's overrides list only the permissions that apply to its type, text or voice.
- The descriptions are corrected:
  - Time out says the member can only read,
  - View channels no longer claims to cover reading history,
  - Masquerade says it's for bots,
  - Embed links says what members lose without it.

**Done when** a live check gives a throwaway role each missing permission in turn. Each place shows its reason, and none fails with a generic error.

### 5. Text moderation

**Built**:
- **Timeout** in a member's menu: 60 s, 5 min, 10 min, 1 hour, 1 day or 1 week, then "Remove timeout". A small clock shows beside a timed-out name, with the end time as its tooltip.
- **Slow mode** in a text channel's settings: off, 5 s, 10 s, 30 s, 1 min, 5 min, 10 min, 1 hour or 6 hours. The composer counts down to the next allowed message, using Stoat's own slow mode events.
- **Ban** gains "Delete their messages from the last…": none, 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days.
- **Reasons**: kick, ban, timeout, server mute, deafen and move each take an optional reason for the audit log.
- **Other people's nicknames and avatars**: "Change nickname" and "Remove server avatar" in a member's menu, with Manage nicknames or Remove avatars.

**Done when** a live check:
- times `aino` out, so her composer is disabled and she can't send over the API, then removes the timeout,
- sets slow mode to 5 s, so a second message is refused with the wait shown, while `dev-admin`, who bypasses it, isn't,
- bans a throwaway account with 1 hour of deletion, so its recent messages disappear for others, then unbans it,
- changes aino's nickname and puts it back.

### 6. Deleting many messages

**Built**: "Select messages" in a channel for those with Manage messages. Click messages to tick them, up to 100, then "Delete N messages" behind one confirmation. Messages older than 7 days can't be ticked, with the reason as a tooltip.

**Done when** a live check ticks and deletes 3 messages, and another member sees them go.

### 7. The audit log

**Built**: an "Audit log" section in server settings, for those with View audit logs:
- newest first: who did what to whom, when, and the reason,
- for member edits, what changed, such as "server muted" or "timed out until 18:00",
- filters by who did it and by action,
- 50 entries a page, loaded as you scroll, at most one page a second so the shared limit isn't spent,
- a note that Stoat keeps 30 days.

**Done when** the actions from tasks 3 to 6 appear with their reasons, and the filters narrow the list.

### 8. Reports

Stoat's own reports reach no one, so Concord's layer takes them.

**Built**:
- **Reporting**: "Report message" and "Report user" in their menus, with a reason from a short list and optional notes.
- **The store** keeps who reported what, when and why, and a copy of the reported message's text, so the evidence survives its deletion. That copy is the one exception to the layer's rule of storing no Stoat data. It's dropped 90 days after the report is closed.
- **Who sees them**:
  - a report from a server channel goes to that server's moderators, meaning those with Manage messages, in a "Reports" section of its server settings,
  - a report from a DM, or about a user, goes to the app's admins on `ADMIN_USER_IDS`, on a "Reports" page in the user menu until the admin panel exists.
- **Handling**: jump to the message, then close the report with a note. Closing doesn't act by itself; the moderator uses the usual actions.
- **Being told**: moderators see a count beside "Reports", fetched every minute.
- **Rules**: the reporter is never shown to the reported person. Each person may file 10 reports an hour.

**Done when** a live check has `dev` report one of aino's messages:
- `dev-admin` sees it in the server's Reports with the text, jumps to the message and closes it,
- `aino`, without Manage messages, can't list reports over the API,
- a DM report reaches the admin list, and only it.

## Decisions

Each is a recommendation that sign-off accepts unless you say otherwise.

1. **Server mute and deafen are enforced by Concord clients** until Stoat is fixed. They take effect at once with no reconnect. Stoat's own client and modified clients can ignore them. Server-side enforcement isn't possible on 0.15.5.
2. **No Disconnect until Stoat fixes it.** The other route needs the LiveKit secret in the web server. A moderator can drag someone into another channel meanwhile. An "AFK" voice channel to drop people into is possible too, but I'd rather not add one.
3. **Reports go through Concord's own layer**, with a copy of the message's text kept as evidence for 90 days after closing, against the layer's rule of storing no Stoat data.
4. **Server reports go to members with Manage messages, and DM and user reports to the app admins.**
5. **Dragging is an extra beside the "Move to" menu**, which is the accessible way.
6. **Webhooks stay out.** Their permission stays hidden until bots and integrations get their own milestone.
7. **Lengths**: Discord's timeout lengths and slow mode steps, as listed.
8. **Order**:
   - tasks 1 to 3 first, the voice moderation you asked for,
   - then 4, permissions that work, which touches everyone,
   - then 5 to 8, text moderation, the audit log and reports,
   - then 9 and 10.

   Tasks 4 to 7 don't depend on the voice work, so a second agent could take them in parallel.

**Belongs elsewhere**, for the record:
- **Disabling an account app-wide** and **instance bans**: Stoat has no route for either, so they need the root-side runner in the admin panel's plan.
- **Automatic moderation**, such as word filters: Stoat has none, and a client-side filter would be easy to get around.
