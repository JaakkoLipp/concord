# M4.6 plan: UI motion and design parity

Status: draft for Jaakko's sign-off, revised after a Sonnet challenger review. Results go in `docs/m4.6-results.md`.

The target is [design/discord-ui-motion-spec.md](design/discord-ui-motion-spec.md): Discord's motion, popups and layout, measured from a recording. Its section 9 is the acceptance checklist. The roadmap entry in [milestones.md](milestones.md) holds the token mapping, the deferred list and the three settled decisions:
- cyan `--accent` stays,
- text keeps its contrast,
- settings becomes a modal over the app.

**A guide, not a copy**: behaviour, timings and layout follow the spec. Colours stay Concord's. Where the spec and M4.2's motion rules differ, the spec wins.

Working agreement, as before: each finished task is committed and pushed after `pnpm verify`, deployed to `/app`, and checked with `routes.ts`. Agents work on the local stack.

## Tasks

The order is the roadmap's, except that task 16a, settings over a mounted shell, moves up to follow task 5. It's the riskiest change, and later settings tasks build on it.

A new live check, `tools/live/src/parity.ts`, grows a step with each task. It reads sizes and positions from the DOM and timings from `getComputedTiming()`, one step per line of spec section 9. `motion.ts` keeps checking what may animate.

### 0. Probes

On the local stack:
- **Settings over a mounted shell**, in a throwaway branch:
  - `pushState` from the channels layout, with the modal drawn from `page.state`,
  - Back closing it,
  - a reload at `/app/settings/voice` falling back to the last channel and reopening the modal,
  - Back with unsaved changes. A shallow pop skips `beforeNavigate`, so the guards must be called directly,
  - Esc while a menu or popover is open over settings,
  - every window key handler under the modal: the dock's mute and deafen keys, the composer's Ctrl+E, and the context menu's, including while Voice settings records a keybind,
  - phone width.
- **Zero durations**: does a Svelte outro with `duration: 0` still fire `onoutroend`, so `Dialog`'s `closed()` runs? Does a 0 ms transition show in `getAnimations()` at all?
- **Scrollbars**: a thumb shown only while its own scroller is hovered, not its ancestors, in Chromium and Firefox. Chromium ignores `::-webkit-scrollbar` wherever `scrollbar-width` or `scrollbar-color` applies.
  - The probe picks one approach and finds out whether a headless check can read its state. Playwright's Chromium hides scrollbars, so likely only a computed `scrollbar-color` is readable.

**Done when** each has an answer in m4.6-results.md, and the throwaway branch is gone.

- **`--dur-hover` becomes `--dur-state`.** After task 3, its users are toggle knobs, speaking rings and the toggle track, none of them hover.
- **client.md section 9**, rules 1 to 8, the rail line and the reduced-motion line in section 11:
  - hover is instant, with no colour fades,
  - tooltips fade without growing, and the profile popout opens without motion,
  - settings closes with a fade and scale,

- **A colour lint**: a new `tools/lint/colours.ts`, in `pnpm verify`, fails on a hex, `rgb()` or `hsl()` colour in CSS: `.css` files and components' `<style>` blocks, outside `tokens.css`. Strings such as `name#1234` aren't CSS, so they don't trip it. The Avatar mask and RoleEditor's role colour presets are data, so they're exempt by name.
- **The motion lint** allows `grid-template-rows` in the settings nav only. Its ban on colour transitions comes with task 3, which removes the last of them.

**Done when** `pnpm verify` passes with the colour lint, which fails on a planted hex colour in a component's style.

### 2. Reduced motion

- Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, every `--dur-*` token becomes 0, fades included. The JS helpers read the same tokens, so they follow.
- The spinner, from task 15, keeps its own token and keeps turning. The typing dots and the ringing pulse stay stopped.
- **The jump highlight**, `--dur-flash`, would vanish at 0, and it's the only sign of where a jump landed. Under reduced motion it shows as a static highlight for 2 s instead.
- **`motion.ts` is rewritten for reduce mode.** About 20 of its steps expect something to animate under reduce. They become "nothing animates", the "keeps its content while closing" checks run in normal mode only, and the recorder reads `getComputedTiming()`.

**Done when** `motion.ts` in reduce mode records no animation longer than 0 ms, dialogs still close and return focus, and a jump to a message still shows its highlight. Tasks 5 and 15 add their own reduce-mode steps.

### 5. Spring scroll

`springScrollTo` in `packages/ui/src/motion.ts`:
- a critically damped spring at 12.75 rad/s,
- keeps its velocity when a new target comes mid-scroll,
- stops on wheel or touch,
- jumps under reduced motion.

It uses the spring's closed form, recomputed on a retarget, rather than stepping by frame. So dropped frames don't stretch it, and a unit test can check it.

**Done when** the unit test shows a 600 px and a 1,200 px scroll both reaching 99 % of the way in 490 to 550 ms, and a retarget with no jump in velocity. Task 17's live step measures it in the browser.

### 16a. Settings over a mounted shell

The routing half of the roadmap's decision 3, as the probe settled it. Recommended shape:
- **Opening**: one `openSettings()` helper replaces the five `goto` call sites: the dock's gear and its Voice settings item, Edit profile, and `openServerSettings` and `openChannelSettings` in `admin/menu.ts`.
  - It pushes the settings URL as shallow state, so the shell, the call and the conversation stay mounted under it.
  - The URLs don't change.
- **Reloads and links**: the three settings routes become openers. They load the last channel, or one in that server, then open the modal over it.
- **Closing**: Back and Esc both close it. Esc defers to an open menu, popover or dialog first.
- **Unsaved changes**: a shallow Back can't be cancelled, so the five editors that guard with `beforeNavigate` register with the modal instead. On Back with unsaved changes, the modal pushes its entry again and asks. Closing the tab still warns.
- **Under the modal**:
  - The shell's overlay hosts, such as toasts, confirms, menus and the emoji picker, move out of the `.shell` div, which then goes inert as a whole.
  - The profile popout closes when settings opens.
  - Settings' own copies of the confirm dialog, toaster and admin dialogs go, since the shell already hosts them.
  - Window key handlers that the probe found live are gated on settings being open.
- **Focus** returns to the opener, or to the menu's trigger when a menu item opened it.
- **Phone width**: at 800 px or less settings stays full-screen, as now.

**Done when** a live check, clicking through the UI rather than visiting URLs:
- opens each kind of settings from a channel during a call, and finds the same call view element and the conversation's scroll position on close,
- closes it with Back and with Esc, and with Esc closes an open menu first,
- edits a server's name, presses Back and gets the unsaved-changes question,
- finds focus back on the gear, and Tab never reaching the shell while it's open,
- records a keybind in Voice settings without muting.

A reload at a settings URL reopens it over a channel. The checks that visit settings URLs pass unchanged: `profile.ts`, `m2c.ts`, `roles.ts`, `moderation.ts`, `overrides.ts`, `tags.ts`, `voice-channel.ts`, `admin.ts`, `layer.ts` and `emoji.ts`.

## Decisions

Each is a recommendation that sign-off accepts unless you say otherwise.

1. **Settings uses shallow routing**, with the three routes kept as openers for reloads and links. A plain store-driven modal is simpler, but it loses Back closing it, the links and the URLs the live checks use.
2. **At 800 px or less, settings stays full-screen**, with no inset and no accordion. An inset modal on a phone would waste the screen.
3. **No colour transitions at all**, enforced by the lint, except the toggle track. Hover opacity fades are caught by the live check instead.
4. **Attachments keep the blurred thumbhash** under the fade, rather than the spec's flat average colour. It looks better and is already built. Embed images have no thumbhash, so they start on a plain surface.
5. **With no custom status, the user panel's second line shows presence**, so the slide always reveals the username.
6. **The accordion lists sections only where a page has them**, today Voice and video and Profile.
7. **No settings search box and no profile block** in the sidebar. User settings has three pages, and the title already names the server or channel. The red Log out, the version and the accordion stay.
8. **The skeleton shows on first visits and after reconnects only**, since loaded channels are kept. The member list's headers come from the server's hoisted roles while members load.
9. **The action bar shows the top three of M4.5's use-ranked reactions**, not the last three used.
