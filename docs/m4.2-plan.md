# M4.2 plan: motion and polish

Status: signed off by Jaakko on 2026-10-15, with the recommendations, and asking for Discord's style: slight, never in the way, polish only. The values below are toned down to that. Revised after a challenger review. Implements M4.2 in [milestones.md](milestones.md).

## Where we are

Durations are already tokens, but motion is sparse and uneven:
- Menus and popovers open with a 0.98 scale from their centre and vanish on close. Dialogs and the image viewer have no motion at all. All of them unmount the moment they close, so today there is nothing to animate out.
- The server rail's pill animates `height`, and speaking rings and the ringing avatar animate `box-shadow`. Both repaint on every frame.
- With reduced motion, every duration is 0 ms, and four components switch their animations off entirely. The milestone asks for fades to stay.
- The easing tokens are proposals from M1: an ease-out, and an ease-in that nothing uses.

## The two sources

A Sonnet agent read both online. Nothing was installed or cloned.
- **Emil Kowalski's skills** in nexu-io/open-design (`skills/emil-design-eng`, `skills/review-animations`). The repo is Apache-2.0, but these folders carry an MIT licence of their own. We paraphrase and cite, and copy no text.
- **Apple's Human Interface Guidelines**: Motion, and Accessibility for reduced motion. They give principles, not numbers.

They agree on the main rules: motion has a purpose, frequent actions move less, motion never makes anyone wait, and a fade is the safe fallback. Kowalski gives the numbers. Under reduced motion, Apple also drops scaling, so we follow Apple there.

## The spec, in short

This goes into client.md section 9, task 1 below.

1. **Only `transform` and `opacity` move.** That includes `translate`, `scale` and `rotate`.
   - Colours may fade on hover, and the level meter's `clip-path` stays.
   - Nothing animates size or position (`height`, `width`, `top`, `margin`, `padding`, `inset`), and nothing uses `transition: all`.
2. **Frequency decides.**
   - **Hundreds of times a day**: no motion. This covers anything opened from the keyboard, switching channels, new messages and arrow keys in lists.
   - **Tens of times a day**: a colour change or a small press, at most. This covers hover and buttons.
   - **Occasional**: the full enter and exit. This covers menus, popovers, dialogs, toasts, the voice panel and people joining a call.
3. **Enter slower than exit.** Every exit takes `--dur-exit`. Pages, such as settings, are the exception: they close instantly.
4. **Things grow from where they came from.** A menu scales from the corner nearest its trigger, and a tooltip from the side facing its target. Dialogs stay centred.
5. **Slight, as Discord's.** No enter starts from nothing, and none travels far. Popovers, dialogs and call tiles start at 0.98 scale with opacity 0, and rises are 4 px (`--space-2`).
6. **Two curves.**
   - `--ease-out` is `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`. It covers every enter, exit, press and hover.
   - `--ease-in-out` is `cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)`. It covers things moving on screen, such as the phone drawer, and the typing dots.
   - `linear` is used only for the level meter. There is no ease-in, and no springs.
7. **Durations**:

   | Token | Value | For |
   |---|---|---|
   | `--dur-hover` | 120 ms | colour on hover |
   | `--dur-press` | 120 ms | the press scale |
   | `--dur-popover` | 150 ms | menus, popovers, tooltips entering |
   | `--dur-modal` | 200 ms | dialogs, settings and the drawer entering |
   | `--dur-toast` | 200 ms | toasts and the incoming call card entering |
   | `--dur-exit` | 100 ms | every exit |
   | `--dur-meter` | 60 ms | the level meter's smoothing, today's `--dur-press` |
   | `--dur-loop` | 1.2 s | the typing dots and the ringing pulse |
   | `--dur-flash` | 2 s | a jumped-to message's highlight |

8. **Reduced motion keeps the fades.** One token, `--motion`, is 1 normally and 0 under reduced motion.
   - Every scale and movement is written so that it collapses to none at 0. For example, a popover's start is `scale(calc(1 - 0.02 * var(--motion)))`.
   - Opacity and colour still fade at the same durations.
   - The phone drawer, toggle knobs, category chevrons and the rail's pill snap into place instead of sliding, and looping pulses stop.
   - The four component rules that switch animations off are removed.
9. **Exits keep their content.** A closing menu, popover, dialog or toast stays on screen with what it showed while it fades out. It ignores the pointer while it leaves, and focus returns at once. People leaving a toast stack slide the others into place with `transform`.

## Tasks

1. **The motion spec**: the rules above, written into client.md section 9 with the sources cited. The "Motion" line saying all durations go to 0 ms is replaced.
   Done when the section reads as the summary above.
2. **Tokens and a guard**:
   - the new tokens go in tokens.css, `--ease-in` is removed, and `--motion` replaces the zeroed durations in base.css,
   - the four `animation: none` rules go,
   - the meter, typing dots, ring pulse and message flash move onto their own tokens,
   - a lint in `tools/lint/`, run by `pnpm verify`, reads `<style>` blocks, `.css` files and `tooltip.ts`'s inline styles. It fails on:
     - a raw duration or `cubic-bezier` outside tokens.css,
     - `transition: all`,
     - a transition or keyframe of a layout property from rule 1's list.

   Done when verify passes with the lint in, and fails on a planted `transition: height 1s`.
3. **Menus, popovers and tooltips**:
   - **Exit**: each primitive leaves with a Svelte `out:` transition. Svelte freezes an outgoing block's content, so a caller clearing its state on close doesn't empty the box. This is checked first. If it doesn't hold, the primitive keeps a copy of what it last showed. While leaving, the box is `inert`.
   - **Origin**: Menu works out the corner from its anchor or click point against where it was placed, since `place()` can clamp it. Popover uses its side after clamping. The shared tooltip sets its origin each time it shows, and doesn't animate when a neighbouring tooltip was showing a moment before.
   - **Keyboard**: a menu opened while its trigger has `:focus-visible` appears without motion, using the test `tooltip.ts` already uses.

   This covers the context menus, server menu, profile popouts, emoji picker and status menu. Done when:
   - each enters from the right corner,
   - each leaves with a fade and keeps its content,
   - a menu opened with Enter on a focused button appears at once.
4. **Dialogs, the image viewer and settings**:
   - **Dialog**: it enters with a fade and a scale from 0.98 at `--dur-modal`, and the backdrop only fades.
   - **Closing**: Dialog keeps its content under an `out:` transition and calls the native `close()` when the transition ends, with a timeout as a fallback. This works the same in Chrome, Firefox and Safari, without `@starting-style` or `allow-discrete`.
   - **Image viewer**: it uses the same mechanism, with its manual `display: none` removed.
   - **Settings**: a page, not a dialog. It fades in at `--dur-modal` when opened and closes instantly, as rule 3 says. A change of section doesn't replay the fade.

   Done when dialogs open and close smoothly in Chrome and Firefox, and moving between settings sections doesn't fade.
5. **Press and hover**:
   - `Button` and the call controls scale to 0.98 while pressed,
   - rows and icon buttons get colour only, since they are frequent,
   - hover colours apply only to fine pointers, through `@media (hover: hover)`, so taps on a phone don't leave rows lit.

   Done when a button press visibly gives, and a phone tap leaves no hover colour behind.
6. **Voice and the rest**:
   - **Voice panel**: it fades in with a small rise inside its own box when joining, and fades out when leaving. The dock's row changes height at once. The sidebar's bottom snaps up or down, which is accepted, since animating that row's height is a layout animation.
   - **Call tiles**: tiles added after the call view first shows fade and scale in, and leaving tiles fade and scale out. Switching between grid and focus remounts every tile, so it doesn't animate.
   - **Speaking rings and the ringing avatar**: an opacity ring, instead of `box-shadow`.
   - **The rail's pill**: it scales with `scaleY`, instead of animating `height`.
   - **Toasts and the incoming call card**: they leave with a fade, and the remaining toasts slide into place.

   New messages don't animate. Done when none of these animate anything but rule 1's properties, and switching grid and focus doesn't flash.
7. **A check, and the review**: `tools/live/src/motion.ts` opens each surface above on the local stack.
   - It records every `transitionrun` and `animationstart`, and reads each animation's properties: `transitionProperty` for transitions, and `effect.getKeyframes()` for animations, Svelte's included.
   - It checks that only rule 1's properties animate, and that with reduced motion emulated only `opacity` and colours do.
   - It checks that no frame stalls longer than 50 ms. Headless has no GPU, so this is a lower bound.

   Then the client is deployed to production for Jaakko. Done when the check and `pnpm verify` pass.

One commit per task. Tasks 3 to 6 each get screenshots taken mid-animation where it helps.

## Decisions

Each comes with my recommendation.

1. **New messages don't animate.** They arrive hundreds of times a day, both sources say no, and Discord doesn't either.
2. **Press scale on buttons and call controls only.** Not on rows or icon buttons, which are frequent. Discord has none at all, so this is a small step past it.
3. **Kowalski's curves replace M1's proposal.** They are stronger, with a quicker start and a longer settle, so short durations still read as smooth.
4. **Reduced motion keeps fades**, instead of the current 0 ms everywhere, as the milestone asks.
5. **Menus opened from the keyboard appear instantly**, as Kowalski advises for keyboard-initiated actions.
6. **Settings fades in without a scale, and closes instantly.** It is a full page, and a scale on a full page reads as a zoom.
7. **The sidebar snaps when the voice panel comes or goes.** The panel itself fades. Smoothing the snap would mean animating a height, which rule 1 rules out.
