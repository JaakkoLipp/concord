## 0. Probes

- **Settings:** a shallow push to the settings URL keeps the underlying shell mounted. Back closes it without calling `beforeNavigate`; dirty Back must restore the entry and ask directly. A tested opener sequence navigates to the base route before pushing settings. Reload currently reaches the standalone route, so task 16a must turn the three routes into openers over the remembered channel. See [settings probe evidence](m46-settings-probes.md) for tested behavior and the keyboard audit.
- **Zero durations:** Chromium and Firefox both close the real custom-status dialog after its zero-duration Svelte outro. Its `onoutroend` callback runs. A bare zero-duration WAAPI animation is already finished and absent from `getAnimations()` immediately and on the next frame. The live recorder must inspect computed timings and permit animations that never enter that list.
- **Scrollbars:** both engines compute a transparent thumb initially and while hovering the parent outside the child, then a coloured thumb over the child scroller. Use the standard `scrollbar-color` hover rule in both engines; avoid competing prefixed thumb colours. Headless checks read computed style; Jaakko's final recording checks painted shape and visibility. See [motion probe evidence](m46-motion-probes.md).
